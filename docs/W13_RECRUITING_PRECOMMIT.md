# Week 13 recruiting precommit

Committed 3 November 2026, before any outreach message is sent. This file exists because a promise about
future behavior ("I will not cast a user I know will hit a wall") is not a design decision — it is the
same failure shape as `CORE_ONLY` being a UI-only flag instead of a server-side guard (see `DECISIONS.md`,
"Mechanical test pass," 1 Nov 2026). The fix there was to make the rule unable to be bypassed by accident.
The fix here is to make the recruiting criteria unable to be quietly redefined after I see who responds,
by writing them down first and locking them behind an append-only git history, the same discipline Cuadre
applies to a supplier's data.

## Target roles (decided before outreach, not adjusted after)

Job titles or functions I will approach, in this order of priority:
- Calidad (quality)
- Cumplimiento (compliance)
- Comercio exterior (foreign trade / import-export)
- Control de inventario (inventory control)

These are chosen because they are the roles closest to Gate 3's declared user: a compliance reviewer
reconciling packaging paperwork, not a street-level vendor photographing a box.

## The rule I am bound by

I do not pre-screen anyone, after they respond, for how comfortable they look with the tool, how
technical they seem, or how likely they appear to succeed with it. A response from the target roles
above is sufficient; I do not filter further based on an impression of who will make the test look good.

## Order of operations

1. LinkedIn cold outreach to the target roles above, at small-to-mid producers and importers in Mexico.
   Budget: roughly 30-40 messages, expecting a 5-10% response rate (3-4 real conversations).
2. If needed, referrals through Extinción Cero board contacts who run or supply small businesses, to
   someone in one of the target roles at a company they deal with. That person remains a stranger to me
   (Week 13 Forbidden Zone: 3 of 5 must be strangers), even though the introduction came from someone I know.
3. Only after Wednesday, if cold outreach and referrals have produced zero responses: widen to adjacent
   roles who touch packaging specs occasionally (purchasing, logistics), stated here in advance as the
   fallback, not improvised after the fact.

## What would violate this

- Choosing a tester because they already looked like they'd succeed.
- Dropping a tester who responded, in favor of someone else, without logging why here first.
- Redefining "stranger who does this job" to mean something looser than the four target roles above,
  after seeing who actually answered.

Any such change, if it becomes necessary, gets logged here with a reason and a date, the same way Cuadre
logs a decision and a reason for every exception. It does not get made silently.
