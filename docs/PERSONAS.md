# PERSONAS: Cuadre

Week 12 · **Emilio Gómez González** · Team 7 · version 0.1 · 1 November 2026

**Read this first.** These are two invented archetypes built from my Week 11 research and the Week 11 persona test. They are not real people, and the quotes are written in their voice by me; none is something a real person said. No real coordinator has used Cuadre yet. Version 0.1 is replaced by version 0.2 after the Gate 1 interview and the Week 13 user tests, and every change is logged at the bottom. Persona tests in a fresh chat impersonate these two.

## Persona 1: Karla, the coordinator (primary)

- **Name and age:** Karla, 34.
- **Context:** regulatory and quality coordinator at a mid-size importer of packaged home-care products in Mexico, about 60 SKUs, eight suppliers. Packaging data arrives as Excel files by email plus photos of labels. No ERP or PLM. A second person can review on busy weeks. She reads dense documents carefully, is short on time, and distrusts tools that change her data silently.
- **The need, in her voice (invented):** "When a supplier changes a lid from 24 to 21 grams I need to know, decide which number is true, and be able to show later who decided and why. I don't want to claim anything about recycling that no document supports."
- **What she does today:** keeps one master spreadsheet, compares the new supplier file with the old one by eye or with filters, and writes notes in cell comments or in email threads. How long this takes per cycle is not measured; it is a number to collect from a real person.
- **What she would pay:** unknown. No figure is claimed. To test: ask what she pays today in staff time and whether her company pays for any packaging-data tool.
- **What would make her quit:** (1) a screen that decides or "fixes" a value without asking; (2) having to re-key data she already has in a file; (3) any wording that says a material is verified recycled when she has no document; (4) an export she cannot explain to an auditor; (5) a Spanish interface that reads like a translation.

## Persona 2: Mauricio, the reviewer (secondary)

- **Name and age:** Mauricio, 46.
- **Context:** quality and compliance manager at the same kind of company. Approves what Karla prepares, signs off before anything leaves the company, and is the person asked when an auditor or a customer questions a number. He has little time and reads summaries first.
- **The need, in his voice (invented):** "Before I approve, I want to see what changed, who decided it and the reason, and which values have no document behind them."
- **What he does today:** reads the master spreadsheet and Karla's email summary, and asks her about anything odd. He has no record of why each change was accepted.
- **What he would pay:** unknown. No figure is claimed. To test with a real reviewer.
- **What would make him quit:** (1) approving a cycle without seeing the undecided or unsupported items; (2) an export that does not say who decided and whether the same person also approved (Cuadre version 1 has one account per workspace and says "revisión por una sola persona"); (3) an interface that hides what the tool does not have.

## Known limits of these personas

- Both are single archetypes; neither is a market segment.
- The second persona exists because version 1 cannot enforce a second reviewer, and that limit has to be visible in the product.
- Suppliers and waste collectors are not personas. Cuadre holds no data about them.
